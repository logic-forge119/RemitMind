Machine Learning Architecture

The 4 AI Engines Behind RemitMind

Designed strictly in accordance with the ML Specification: statistical models for numbers, strict business rules in rules.py, and grounded language for humans.

1. Rate & Send-Time Forecaster

Evaluates 14-day rolling corridor rate movements using moving averages and linear drift models. Outputs 5-day expected rates, optimal dispatch window, and volatility-derived confidence scores.

Algorithm: Linear Trend + MA Target: ≥ 8% Fee Saved

2. Hybrid Isolation Forest

Unsupervised anomaly detection trained on 5,000 synthetic transfers. Examines 30-day amount z-scores, velocity, new device fingerprints, and receiver clustering. Blended with deterministic rule bonuses for zero auto-blocks.

Algorithm: Isolation Forest Recall: ≥ 70% at Top 10%

3. Agent Demand Predictor

Forecasts 7-day physical cash-out demand for 20 rural agent points. Factors in days-to-festival (Eid 2.5x multiplier), day-of-week cycles, and rolling means to prevent agent insolvency.

Algorithm: Gradient Boosted Regressor Target: ≤ 20% MAPE

4. Grounded LLM Explainer

Takes strict structured JSON only (scores, reason codes, numbers). Translates math into plain Bangla for receivers and actionable narratives for risk analysts. Features zero-downtime template fallback.

Input: Strictly Structured JSON Fallback: Template Engine
Governance & Ethics

Responsible AI by Design

Strict compliance with responsible AI principles: building trust through explainability, human agency, and transparent auditing.

100% Synthetic Data

Zero real PII or bank records used. Generated using reproducible random seed with 5,000 synthetic transfers across 90 days.

Zero Unchecked Auto-Blocks

AI cannot autonomously deny funds. Medium and high scores are routed to human analysts who retain full discretion.

Disaggregated Fairness Auditing

Dedicated endpoint /metrics/fairness monitors alert rates across corridors and ticket sizes to eliminate bias.

Analyst Feedback Loop

Analyst decisions record is_fraud_label into the database, powering supervised retraining without cold-start problems.
